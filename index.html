How to use the Booking Planner

🌍 Pick your time zone

Use Show times in at the top to choose Copenhagen or Singapore. All times on the page switch to that zone. Your choice is remembered on this computer.

➕ Book a time

In the Add booking bar, choose your name, the day, and the start and end time, then press Add. If the end time is earlier than the start, the booking runs past midnight into the next day.

✏️ Change or delete a booking

Click a booking. In the window that opens you can change the name, day or times and press Save, or press Delete.

↔️ Move a booking

Drag a booking to another time or day, and let go. It snaps to 15-minute steps. Press Esc while dragging to cancel.

📋 Copy a booking

Hold Ctrl while you drag and let go — the original stays and a copy is made. Or click the booking and use Copy to a different day (same start time and length).

📅 Weeks

You can book today and the next 13 days. Week 1 always starts with today (yellow, first column); Next week shows the 7 days after that. The red line shows the time right now, and the page opens scrolled to it.

📏 Rules

  • Only one person can book a given time. If a time is taken, you'll see a red message saying who has it.
  • Back-to-back is fine (one ends at 14:00, the next starts at 14:00).
  • A booking can be up to 24 hours long.
  • Old bookings are removed automatically about a day after they end.

🟢 "In use" / "Free" banner

The bar under the title shows whether someone has plogenius.com open right now, or that it's free and whose booking is next. It turns "free" about 3 minutes after plogenius is closed.

For this to work, each person installs a small helper once:

  1. Install the free Tampermonkey extension for your browser (from the Chrome Web Store or Edge Add-ons).
  2. Open this link and click Install:
    plogenius helper script
  3. Open plogenius.com. The first time, it asks who you are — type your number.

If nothing happens on plogenius, click the Tampermonkey icon: it may ask you to switch on a setting (in Chrome: "Allow User Scripts" on the extension's Details page).

👥 Shared

Everyone who opens this link sees the same bookings, and changes show up for everyone within a few seconds — no need to refresh. The label at the top says Live when this is working.
